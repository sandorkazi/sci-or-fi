Star Trek: Sci vs Fi, season by season

Each season is scored on two independent axes. Sci is how much the episode text turns on technical and scientific vocabulary; Fi is how much it turns on god-like power, prophecy and reality-bending. Scores are computed per episode from a source scope, then averaged per season; bubble size is the measured 95% confidence interval of that average, never a hand-set value. Covers 15 series, 14 films and 69 seasons; 3 sources in scope (tvmaze + wikipedia + memory-alpha) — toggle them on the right and the chart recomputes. Films have no TVmaze entry and are plotted with a neutral marker; their text comes from the same wiki sources, looked up by article title rather than search. Fi is a two-coder composite only where a franchise has a curated entity list (1 of 29 works); elsewhere it is lexicon-only, and the table says which. The table's text basis column shows how many episodes each source actually resolved and the median words behind a measurement — several series rest on thin summaries, and that is stated rather than hidden.

Rubric, uncertainty and provenance

Sci - scientific / engineering rigour

How much an episode's text turns on technical, scientific or engineering vocabulary, as opposed to being set dressing.

  • technical-lexicon-density — coder lexicon, 57 terms, weight 1.0, saturates at 20.0

Fi - myth / magic / reality-bending

How much an episode's text turns on god-like power, prophecy, magic-equivalent action or reality-breaking storytelling.

  • mythic-lexicon-density — coder lexicon, 59 terms, weight 1.0, saturates at 9.0
  • numinous-entity — coder entity, curated entity list (franchise:entities), weight 1.0, saturates at 6.0

Saturation values are calibrated on the reference corpus (Star Trek: TOS, 79 episodes) as the 95th percentile of episode-level density, so a season scoring 10 is a genuine corpus-tail outlier rather than a saturation artefact. Raw densities are always published alongside scores.

Uncertainty. ci95-mean: 95% CI of the episode-level mean (z=1.96), inflated ×1.5 below 5 episodes. Measured from the episode distribution, never asserted.

Not yet coded (rubric-v3, needs a judgement coder rather than a lexicon): resolution-mechanism, methodological-framing, internal-consistency, pseudoscience-handwave, prophecy-driven-plot, magic-equivalent-action, reality-or-format-bending, literal-afterlife, absurdist-tonal-break
